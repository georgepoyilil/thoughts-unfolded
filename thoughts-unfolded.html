A gentle next step

How might I work with this feeling?

A feeling can be information, not an instruction. As you write, Thoughts, Unfolded can offer a few possibilities to consider.

  • Name the feeling in your own words.
  • Notice what it brings up in your body and what it urges you to do.
  • Choose one small, kind next step that fits your situation.

These are reflection prompts, not treatment or a diagnosis. Keep what fits and leave the rest.
